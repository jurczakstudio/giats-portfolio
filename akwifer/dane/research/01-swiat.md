# 01 – What the best water-well / borehole drilling websites do (world scan)

Research date: 2026-10-07. Method: web search plus WebFetch of each page listed below. Everything in sections 1, 2 and 4 was seen on the fetched page unless it is marked **[inferred]**. Fetch summaries are text-only, so I could not judge visual design (colours, motion) directly. When I say a page has "no X", that means X did not appear in the fetched text.

**Overall finding:** No drilling site turned up on Awwwards, Dribbble or Behance (searched, nothing found). This niche is mostly plain WordPress, GoDaddy or Wix sites, many of them broken: typos, template placeholders, counters showing "0". The best sites stand out because of **offer design** (fixed-price packages, water guarantees, start-up bundles, memberships, depth lookups), not visual polish. A demo that combines the best offer patterns with good modern design would be ahead of nearly every real site we saw.

## 2. Recurring patterns, ranked (how common × how effective)

Ranking is my judgement from the sample. **C** = how often seen across 25 sites; **E** = how much it seems to help, based on the cited source or **[inferred]**.

1. **Years / generations / wells-drilled counters in or near the hero.** C: almost all. E: high. Best version puts concrete numbers in the headline itself (Austin: "66 Years. 13,000+ Wells."; Barlow: 9,500 wells / 6,000 pumps / 800 geo). Weak version: "Over 50 years" and nothing else. Seen on #1, #7, #9, #11, #15, #18.
2. **Click-to-call as the main CTA, plus a "free estimate / free quote" button.** C: almost all. E: high for urgent pump work. Agency Centergrowth: "Pump repair and replacement is the opposite: urgent and recurring … a phone that gets answered" (https://centergrowth.com/well-drilling-marketing).
3. **Licence, insurance and association logos** (NGWA, state associations, BDA, ISO, Safecontractor, WRAS). C: high. E: medium–high. Centergrowth lists "licensing, bonding, local aquifer knowledge, warranty terms, and water test results" as the trust signals that matter because buyers "cannot inspect the work before committing".
4. **24/7 or same-day emergency messaging, often in a top banner.** C: about 60%. E: high. Best example: SC Well Service, "No Water? Same-day emergency service available." Unsourced agency claim that emergency well terms bring the "highest ROI" (https://clicksgeek.com/industries/well-drilling/), so treat it as indicative.
5. **Service-area / town pages.** C: about 40% of the better US/DE sites (#1, #2, #3, #4, #8, #13). E: high for SEO, and recommended by agencies ("pages for every county and community you serve", Centergrowth). SC Well Service has about 85 of them; Brunnenbohrteam about 20.
6. **Review score with a count** ("4.8 from 170+ Google reviews", "4.7 / 206"). C: about 25%. E: high. Mostly on the newer agency-built US sites (#2, #3, #4, #1). Most EU/UK/AU sites show only 1–5 testimonials, which is a clear opening.
7. **Free water test or analysis as a low-commitment lead magnet.** C: about 25% (#1, #3, #7, #10). E: high **[inferred]**: it is the entry offer that leads into a treatment sale.
8. **Financing / "from X per month".** C: about 30% of US sites (#3, #4, #7, #8, #10, #2). E: medium–high. A UCFS lender claims financing "converts more water well consultations into closed deals" (no numbers given; https://www.ucfs.net/...). We found it on no EU site.
9. **Guarantees.** Three types: (a) **water / no-water-no-fee** (Cox UK "No water - NO FEE"; gartenbrunnen-bohren.de "langfristige Wassergarantie"; Swedish quotes typically guarantee a yield, e.g. 1,200–2,400 l/day per byggahus.se forum threads); (b) **well/workmanship warranty** (M&R "5 year guarantee on your new well"; Goodwin lifetime casing-seal warranty in writing); (c) satisfaction guarantee. C: about 30%. E: very high where offered **[inferred]**, because fear of a dry hole is the main objection (a SA news story: "R70k Gone … 200m With No Water", https://briefly.co.za/...).
10. **Maintenance memberships / service contracts.** C: about 20% (#3, #4, #8, #18). E: high for recurring revenue. NGWA backs the "annual checkup" message (wellowner.org).
11. **Published prices: fixed-price packages, per-metre add-ons, cost guides.** C: about 20%, mostly the German garden-well sites (#13–#15) plus B&L (#5) and the SC cost guide (#2). E: high **[inferred]** because it filters out tyre-kickers and builds trust. In DE this is close to a category norm for garden wells.
12. **Interactive tools: depth lookup, cost calculator, ROI/payback, ZIP checker.** C: rare on driller sites (#2, #3, #6, and the SA aggregators #21, #22). E: high as lead magnets: coloradowell.com puts its results behind an email, and ourpower.co.za uses WhatsApp. **This is the biggest gap to exploit.**
13. **Grant / subsidy explainer.** Ireland (#19) puts the council well grant on its homepage. SA/US geothermal sites mention incentives. E: high wherever a subsidy exists **[inferred]**.
14. **Real crew / rig / fleet photos.** C: common but low quality. Fleet as proof (#17, #20). No site we saw used drilling time-lapse, drone video, or interactive geological cross-sections. **Gap.**
15. **Lead form design.** Usually 4–8 fields. Smarter versions: service dropdown + ZIP (#3), property address (#2), "well already nearby?" + photo upload (#13), 3-step wizard (#25), task router (#10). SMS/WhatsApp only on #2 (Text Us) and the SA sites (#21, #22). **Live chat: one site only** (Hanson), and badly done.

Benchmarks (general home services, not well-specific; quote with care):
- WordStream 2026: average Google Ads CPL **$66.69** (https://www.wordstream.com/blog/2026-google-ads-benchmarks). A separate benchmark table found in the same search lists "Home and home improvement" at CPL **$90.92** and a conversion rate of 8.05%. I could not pin down which source that table comes from, so verify it before quoting.
- Median contractor website converts **2–4%** of visitors; top sites **8–12%** (PipelineOn data as cited by https://www.garretthandley.com/contractor-website-conversion-rate-in-2026-why-most-sites-convert-under-3-and-what-to-fix). Vendor-sourced.
- A well-drilling-specific agency page claims site conversion of 3.8%, search CPA $88.50, LSA conversion 12.5% (https://cufinder.io/blog/benchmarks/well-drilling/). Methodology not shown; low confidence.
- No published water-well-driller case study with audited lead numbers was found. The agency pages (Konstruct, Centergrowth, SERPWARS, Elevated Marketing) give only generic or non-well claims.

## 3. Top 10 transferable ideas for the Polish demo

1. **A number-led hero headline plus a dry-hole guarantee badge.** Example: "27 lat. 3 400 studni. Wiemy, co jest pod Twoją działką." with a badge "Gwarancja wody – nie ma wody, nie płacisz" (or a guaranteed yield, e.g. "min. 1 m³/h"). Sources: https://austindrillingsc.com/ , https://coxwaterboreholedrilling.co.uk/ , https://gartenbrunnen-bohren.de/ , https://www.mrwelldrilling.com/
2. **A "Sprawdź swoją działkę" depth and cost lookup.** Enter a town or click the map → typical aquifer depth, typical well depth in the area, price range, and "studnie wykonane w okolicy" pins. Gate the PDF result behind an email or phone. **[inferred]** Polish data could come from PIG-PIB hydrogeological databases (not verified in this research); in a demo, use mock data per town. Sources: https://coloradowell.com/ , https://scwellservice.com/ (Well Depth Calculator) , https://www.ourpower.co.za/tools/borehole-drilling-cost/cape-town
3. **Public fixed-price packages + price per extra metre** (three cards, middle one marked "Najczęściej wybierany"). Example: "Studnia do ogrodu / Studnia do domu / Studnia głębinowa premium – cena ryczałtowa do X m, potem Y zł/mb". Sources: https://www.brunnenbohrteam.de/brunnenbau-preise/ , https://bandlwaterwell.com/pricing , https://www.mcgartenservice.de/
4. **An interactive cost calculator with ROI/payback** (depth slider, purpose: ogród/dom/rolnictwo, pump type, extras such as water test, stacja uzdatniania, pressure tank, electrics) → itemised estimate + "zwrot vs. woda z wodociągu w X lat". Sources: https://www.boreholehub.co.za/ , https://www.ourpower.co.za/tools/borehole-drilling-cost/cape-town , https://scwellservice.com/blog/water-well-cost-2026.html
5. **A "Pakiet Start" bundle**: free site visit + handling of formalities (zgłoszenie / pozwolenie wodnoprawne **[inferred – Polish permit rules not researched here]**) + water analysis + first-year service. Sources: https://www.bryantspump.com/ , https://www.abwelldrillinginc.com/about/financing , https://www.mcgartenservice.de/ (permit handling)
6. **A service membership ("Abonament serwisowy")**: yearly inspection, water test, pump/pressure tank check, 10% off repairs, priority emergency response. Price shown monthly. Sources: https://www.bryantspump.com/ , https://www.skillingsandsons.com/ , https://www.daleswater.co.uk/ , https://wellowner.org/
7. **An emergency lane**: top banner "Brak wody? Przyjazd tego samego dnia" + a task router (Awaria / Nowa studnia / Uzdatnianie / Serwis) + sticky mobile bar Zadzwoń / SMS / WhatsApp / Wycena. Sources: https://scwellservice.com/ , https://capitalwell.com/ , https://www.ourpower.co.za/tools/borehole-drilling-cost/cape-town
8. **A smart multi-step quote form** (3 steps): purpose → location/postcode + "czy sąsiedzi mają studnię? jak głęboką?" → contact + photo upload of the plot or access road. Promise a response time. Sources: https://www.brunnenbohrteam.de/brunnenbau-preise/ , https://www.jensendrilling.com/ , https://www.bryantspump.com/
9. **Subsidy and financing module**: explain any applicable dotacje (gmina/WFOŚiGW/"Moja Woda" **[inferred – Polish programmes not verified here]**) the way Briody explains Irish council grants, plus "od X zł/mies." instalments. Sources: https://briodydrilling.ie/ , https://www.abwelldrillinginc.com/about/financing , https://www.michaelbarlowwelldrilling.com/
10. **Local SEO town pages + proof**: a "Studnie głębinowe [Miasto]" template with local depth facts, a map of completed wells, Google rating with count, and dated reviews from that town. Show the fleet and crew as proof. Media nobody in the sample uses (time-lapse of drilling, drone shot, animated geological cross-section showing the well going down through soil layers as you scroll) would be the visual differentiator **[inferred: gap observed]**. Sources: https://scwellservice.com/ , https://www.brunnenbohrteam.de/brunnenbau-preise/ , https://www.bryantspump.com/ , https://dragondrilling.co.uk/
